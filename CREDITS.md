# Credits

The theme colors come from these projects. Both use the MIT license.

- **Doki Theme** by Unthrottled: https://github.com/doki-theme/doki-theme-vscode
  The 88 Doki themes in `hooks/themes.ts` come from its VS Code extension.
- **termcn** by shadcn-labs: https://github.com/shadcn-labs/termcn
  The termcn themes in `hooks/termcn-themes.ts` come from its theme registry (https://termcn.dev).

The theme names are the names of characters from anime and games. Their owners keep all rights to those characters.
